**More pension, more Pillar 3a, new BVG limits: what 2027 brings.**

## AHV: Two Reasons to Celebrate

December 2026: around 2.6 million pensioners receive the 13th AHV pension payment for the first time, automatically and without having to apply. January 2027: all pensions increase by 1.59%, with the maximum pension rising to CHF 2,560 per month. November 29 remains one to watch: that's when Switzerland votes on a higher VAT rate to help fund it.

## Pillar 3a: Contribute More, Save More

You can now contribute CHF 7,373 (if you have a pension fund) and deduct the full amount from your taxes. An insider tip: since this year, you can make back-payments to close gaps from 2025 and 2026.

## BVG: Small Numbers, Big Impact for SMEs

The entry threshold and coordination deduction are both rising. Anyone earning just above CHF 22,680 who doesn't get a raise could drop out of the pension fund altogether. And the interest rate is worth watching too: the BVG Commission recommends 1.75% instead of today's 1.25%, though the Federal Council has yet to decide.

2. **Update your payroll system** (for employers): the new BVG figures must be in place as of January 1.
