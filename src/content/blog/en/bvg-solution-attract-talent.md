Job applicants are paying closer attention to the overall package: flexible working conditions, development opportunities, company culture – and a convincing occupational pension solution.

A good BVG solution can therefore be far more than a legal obligation. Designed correctly and communicated clearly, it becomes a genuine competitive advantage in the job market.

## The Pension Fund Is an Often Underestimated Employee Benefit

Many companies invest substantial amounts every year in their employees' occupational pension. Yet this benefit is often barely mentioned in recruiting, onboarding, or performance reviews.

That's a missed opportunity. An above-average BVG solution offers employees long-term financial added value. It can lead to higher retirement savings while also improving coverage in the event of disability or death.

For employees, it's not just about how much is paid into the pension fund. Equally important are:

- the amount of insured salary;
- the split of contributions between employer and employees;
- the savings contributions;
- the interest rate on retirement savings;
- the benefits in the event of disability and death;
- how part-time employees are taken into account;
- the flexibility of the pension plan;
- the costs and financial stability of the pension institution.

A good solution therefore looks not just at the price, but at the relationship between cost, benefits, security and long-term value.

## Greater Employer Appeal Without a Classic Salary Race

Companies can't win every competition for talent by offering higher salaries. An above-average pension solution opens up an additional way to position the company as a responsible employer.

For example, a company can:

- cover more than the legally required 50 percent of contributions;
- choose a lower or salary-dependent coordination deduction;
- offer higher savings contributions;
- insure salary components beyond the legal minimum;
- include better benefits in the event of disability and death;
- offer a choice of different savings plans.

Such improvements can make a significant long-term financial difference for employees. At the same time, they show that the company thinks not only about current work performance but also about its employees' future.

## Especially Important for Part-Time Employees

A classic fixed coordination deduction can mean that part-time employees only have a small portion of their income insured through the pension fund. A modern pension solution can adjust the coordination deduction to the level of employment or structure it differently altogether.

This particularly improves the pension situation of people who work part-time due to family responsibilities, further education, caregiving duties, or other reasons.

A part-time-friendly pension plan is therefore not just a financial benefit. It also conveys a clear stance on fairness, equality, and modern working models.

## Good Pension Provision Supports Recruiting

Even in job postings and interviews, the BVG solution can be mentioned as a concrete benefit. General statements like “attractive benefits” aren't enough for this, however.

Clear, verifiable advantages are more convincing:

- We cover 60 percent of your BVG contributions.
- With us, a larger portion of your salary is insured than the legal minimum.
- Our pension solution also takes part-time employees into account appropriately.
- You can choose between several savings plans.

This lets applicants see the concrete added value the offer contains. Experienced specialists and people with long-term career plans in particular notice these differences.

## Employees Only Value What They Understand

A good BVG solution has little effect if nobody understands it. Many employees receive a pension certificate once a year but can barely make sense of the amounts and technical terms listed in it.

Companies should therefore invest not only in better benefits but also in clear communication.

Helpful measures include, for example:

- a brief explanation when joining the company;
- an easy-to-understand summary of the pension plan;
- concrete examples for different life situations;
- digital information sessions;
- personal pension consultations;
- an annual explanation of employer contributions;
- comparisons with the legal BVG minimum.

It's particularly effective to make the employer contribution visible in francs:

“Your employer paid an additional CHF 4,200 into your occupational pension last year.”

This turns an abstract insurance benefit into a tangible part of the overall compensation package.

## A Good BVG Solution Strengthens Employee Loyalty

Occupational pension provision has a long-term effect. Employees see that their employer takes responsibility and thinks beyond next month's paycheck. This can strengthen trust and loyalty.

The pension fund alone obviously won't prevent employees from leaving. But it is an important part of a credible overall offering consisting of:

- fair compensation;
- good working conditions;
- personal development;
- appreciative leadership;
- financial security.

The effect is especially strong when the company regularly reviews the pension solution and involves employees in its ongoing development.

## What Companies Should Check

A professional BVG analysis should answer at least the following questions:

- How does our solution compare to similar companies?
- Are costs and benefits in a good ratio?
- How high is the effective employer contribution?
- Are part-time employees adequately insured?
- How attractive is the plan for specialists and executives?
- Are the benefits in the event of disability and death sufficient?
- How have the interest rate and financial stability developed?
- Do our employees understand the value of the benefits offered?
- Can improvements be achieved within the existing budget?
- Does the solution still fit our company's HR strategy?

Switching pension funds isn't always necessary. Significant improvements can often already be achieved through better plan design or communication.

## Conclusion: From a Mandatory Benefit to a Competitive Advantage

A good BVG solution is more than administration and insurance. It can help attract qualified employees, retain existing talent, and make a company's social responsibility visible.

The key lies in combining three factors:

- a needs-based and fair pension solution;
- a transparent comparison of costs and benefits;
- communication that makes the personal value understandable.

Companies that actively shape their occupational pension provision aren't just investing in their employees' retirement. They're investing in trust, employer appeal, and long-term collaboration.
